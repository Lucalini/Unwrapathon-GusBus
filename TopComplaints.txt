# Common Surfline App Complaints

This document compiles common complaints users express about the Surfline app, 
drawn from reviews and user forums representing surfing community sentiment.

## 1. Poor Forecast Accuracy

Many users say the wave size, quality or timing predictions are regularly off.

- "Half of the time they're wildly off-base for the next day..." (JustUseApp)
- "Forecast doesn't help you at all..." (Trustpilot)

## 2. High Cost / Aggressive Monetisation

Users complain that features previously free are now behind a paywall, and that 
the premium subscription is expensive.

- "The newest update only lets you check the surf report 4 times in a week... 
  Pretty lame seeing how they use normal publicly available data..." (JustUseApp)
- "They've successfully monopolized surf forecasting... exploit surfers into 
  outrageous membership fees." (Trustpilot)

## 3. Too Many Ads / Poor Free-Tier Experience

Free users say they get bombarded with adverts and limited functionality, 
making the app frustrating unless you pay.

- "Ads are terrible, UI sucks... Their models are consistently wrong..." 
  (Trustpilot)
- "Ad riddled and too busy." (Kimola)

## 4. Feature Removal / Limitation of Free Checks

Some users say the app now restricts how many "surf-checks" or cams you can 
view as a free user, reducing usefulness.

- "You can only open Surfline two or three times a day, but once you exceed 
  that, there is no way to access information for any location beyond wave 
  height and quality..." (JustUseApp)
- "The newest update only lets you check... 5 times per week!" (JustUseApp)

## 5. Poor Customer Support / Billing Issues

Complaints about automatic renewals, difficulty cancelling, unresponsive 
support.

- "Duplicate charges... numerous emails... no response." 
  (ProductReview.com.au)
- "I purchased Surfline Premium... weeks later still unable to log on... no 
  correspondence from Surfline or refund." (Better Business Bureau)

## 6. Camera / Live-Cam Functionality Problems

Even for paid users, cams may be down, freeze, or offer poor angles/resolution; 
free users often lose access.

- "Cams just freeze and you are prompted to subscribe unfortunately." 
  (ProductReview.com.au)
- "Had Coastwatch... Surfline is garbage... most of the beaches have cameras... 
  when I try to access beach cams... always an issue." (ProductReview.com.au)

## 7. Data / Model Coverage Poor Outside Major Spots

For smaller/less popular surf breaks, or obscure regions, the forecasts or 
cams may be worse or missing.

- "I live in Cornwall where conditions vary widely... Yet we just receive a 
  daily forecast... Needless to say, it's wildly inaccurate to the point of 
  being meaningless." (Trustpilot)
- "App performance issues... Sometimes nothing works... locations can't be 
  found..." (Kimola)

## 8. Updates Degrade User Experience

Users say recent versions of the app removed beloved features, changed UI/UX 
in worse ways, or tilted heavily toward upselling premium.

- "I've been using this app for years but this last update is just terrible. 
  ... Now, I can only do that 3 times a week with a free account." (JustUseApp)
- "Our relationship... fell flat... the final draining... handlers wanted to 
  charge me just to see you a few times a week..." (JustUseApp)

## 9. Interface / Device Issues

Complaints about the app on certain devices (iPad, etc), poor landscape 
support, search/navigation issues.

- "App works poorly on iPad at all. Doesn't support landscape mode except for 
  cam feeds." (JustUseApp)
- "Login/account issues, crashing/bugs." (JustUseApp)

## 10. Perceived Lack of Value / Better Alternatives

Many users feel they can get similar or better data from free sources, and 
thus the premium doesn't justify its cost.

- "There are tons of surf apps out there for forecasts... The only difference 
  ... was that I could just open Surfline and watch the cam... Now... you have 
  to pay for it." (JustUseApp)
- "I use other free cams now... Do not subscribe it's no better." (Trustpilot)

## 11. Monopoly / Acquisition Concerns

Some users feel Surfline has bought up smaller services (e.g. Magicseaweed) 
and thus reduced competition, leading to worse service.

- "They bought Magicseaweed so now they're nearly the only game in town." 
  (Trustpilot)
- "Bring back MagicSeaweed... surfline stinks..." (ProductReview.com.au)

## 12. Misleading "Observed" Conditions

The app sometimes lists "observed" wave heights or conditions that users on 
scene find incorrect (e.g., claiming larger waves than actual).

- "I am at the southern california beach with ankle deep ripples and Surfline 
  claims a 'observed' 3 feet to 5 feet..." (Trustpilot)

## 13. Inconsistent Global Coverage / Regional Mismatch

For certain regions (Europe, Australia, etc) users feel the models are weak, 
or the forecast grid is too generic.

- "In Australia, particularly Victoria... I think they all suck except the 
  BOM." (Seabreeze.com.au)

## 14. Reduced Stoke / Worse Experience

Some note that instead of helping them catch waves, the app becomes more of 
an annoyance/distraction, because of limitations or upselling.

- "Instead of getting out there and surfing a swell, I often just stared at 
  my phone screen indecisive..." (JustUseApp)

## 15. Limited Free Trial / Difficult Cancelation Trap

Subscriptions may auto-renew; some feel caught by the trial or subscription 
model and get charged unexpectedly.

- "Forgot to cancel. So they charged my card $99 and I got nothing. ... 
  Customer support sux also." (Trustpilot)
